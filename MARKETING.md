# ChangeOrderSeal Marketing Notes

## Positioning

**ChangeOrderSeal** — proof that the extra work was authorized before you did it.

Field techs get burned when a client later says the change was never approved. ChangeOrderSeal turns an on-site scope change into a single, client-verifiable QuantumGuard evidence pack: description, labor/materials estimate, acknowledgment, optional media hashes, timestamp.

No SaaS. Offline. Same verifier as the rest of the Operator Pages suite.

Fits the stack:
- Original quote → VeriQuote
- Extra work authorized → ChangeOrderSeal
- Work performed + photos → FieldSeal / JobSeal / MediaSeal
- Close-out → CloseSeal
- Full job → LifecycleSeal
- Invoice follow-up → Chaser

- Seals the operator-supplied record of the change and acknowledgment.

- Verification is the free QuantumGuard CLI/HTTP.
- No network, no wall-clock inside the seal logic beyond the recorded authorized_at field.
